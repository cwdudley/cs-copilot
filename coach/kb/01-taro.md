# TARO — Execution Framework

Every CS play has four components. When advising on any account situation, think
in TARO: what is the trigger, what action follows, what resource supports it, and
what outcome defines success.

| Component | Definition |
|---|---|
| **T**rigger | The observable signal that initiates action |
| **A**ction | The specific motion the CSM takes |
| **R**esource | What the CSM uses to execute |
| **O**utcome | The measurable result that defines success |

---

## Trigger

An observable, specific signal — not a feeling. Usage drop, executive departure,
NPS score, renewal date approaching, support ticket spike, milestone slip.

A good trigger is one a system could detect. If it cannot be observed
consistently, the play cannot be run consistently.

## Action

The concrete motion: a call, an email, an EBR, an escalation, a feature push, a
stakeholder mapping exercise. One primary action per play — plays that branch
into five actions are programs, not plays.

## Resource

What the CSM brings: success plan, QBR deck, ROI model, escalation path,
benchmark data, executive sponsor. If the resource does not exist yet, building
it is the real work before the play can run.

## Outcome

The measurable result. Not "improve the relationship" — something you can
evaluate as achieved or not. Meeting booked with the Economic Buyer, usage
recovered above threshold, success criteria re-documented, escalation closed.

---

## Using TARO well (model note)

**When it applies:** diagnosis and qualification are done, and they need a next
action. Also when building a repeatable play from a pattern that keeps recurring
across accounts.

**Where TARO connects to the other frameworks:** MEDDPICC gaps make excellent
Triggers. "Economic Buyer unknown with renewal in 80 days" is a trigger; the play
is the motion that closes it; the Outcome is the gap closed.

**Failure modes:**

- Trigger too vague to detect → the play never fires
- Action bundled → nobody knows what to do first
- Resource assumed → the CSM improvises and quality varies per person
- Outcome unmeasurable → you cannot tell whether the play works, so it never
  improves

**Coaching correction:** if someone describes a play and you cannot identify all
four parts, the missing part is usually where it breaks down in practice.
