# Customer Lifecycle and Value Realization

Two tracks run in parallel. They can fall out of sync, and that gap is itself a
risk signal.

---

## Lifecycle — 8 Stages

| Stage | Name | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Pre-Onboard / Handoff | Deal closes; Sales-to-CS handoff; validate handoff quality, seed the outcome catalog, assign CSM |
| 1 | Onboarding | Kickoff through M1–M5 milestones; time-to-value is the key metric |
| 2 | Adoption | Active feature usage expansion; health monitoring; TARO plays on adoption signals |
| 3 | Nurture / Health | Ongoing QBRs, success plan governance, proactive health management |
| 4 | Growth / Expansion | Expansion signal detection, whitespace analysis, AE handoff packaging |
| 5 | Renewal | Risk assessment, forecast modeling, negotiation prep, save strategies |
| 6 | Advocacy | Reference qualification, case study building, advocate burnout protection |
| 7 | Churn | Post-churn forensics, root cause, win-back eligibility, playbook learning |

---

## Value Realization — 5 Stages

| Stage | Name | What is true |
|---|---|---|
| V1 | Outcome Defined | Success criteria and the Outcome & Value Catalog established at handoff |
| V2 | Adoption Confirmed | Core features in use; early value signals visible |
| V3 | Value Demonstrated | Documented evidence linking usage to business outcomes |
| V4 | Value Realized | Customer articulates ROI; renewal and expansion conversations are outcome-anchored |
| V5 | Value Expanded | New use cases, departments, or capabilities delivering additional value |

---

## The sync check

**Lifecycle stage and value stage can be out of sync, and that is the signal.**

An account at Stage 3 (Nurture) still sitting at V1 (Outcome Defined) is a
high-risk condition: months of relationship management with no documented value.
It will renew on goodwill or not at all.

Always assess both tracks. When someone describes an account by lifecycle stage
alone, ask where it sits on value.

**Common dangerous patterns:**

- Stage 3+ still at V1 → no outcome evidence; renewal has nothing to stand on
- Stage 4 (Expansion) below V3 → expanding before demonstrating value on what
  they already bought
- Stage 5 (Renewal) below V3 → you will be defending price with assertions
  instead of evidence

---

## Two-Layer Outcome Model

**Layer 1 — Market-level outcomes.** What the product can deliver for your ICP.
Built pre-sales, owned by RevOps. Generic to the segment.

**Layer 2 — Account-specific value evidence.** What *this* customer has actually
experienced. Built per account, owned by the CSM.

**The rule:** never use Layer 1 language in a renewal or expansion conversation
without Layer 2 evidence behind it.

- Layer 1: "You can achieve a 30% reduction in handle time."
- Layer 2: "You achieved 22% over two quarters — here is the data."

Layer 1 is a proposal. Layer 2 is evidence. Procurement can argue with a
proposal.
