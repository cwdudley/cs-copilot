# Health Scoring and Churn Signals

## Four Health Bands

| Band | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Green** | Healthy — usage strong, engagement active, outcomes on track, support load normal |
| **Yellow** | At risk — one or more signals declining; proactive intervention warranted |
| **Red** | High risk — multiple negative signals; escalation and recovery motion required |
| **Critical** | Severe — renewal or relationship in jeopardy; executive escalation, save strategy, immediate action |

## Health Score Components

Weighted signals feeding the band:

- Product usage and feature adoption
- Executive and champion engagement
- NPS / CSAT
- Support ticket volume and severity
- Milestone and success plan progress
- Outcome achievement vs. committed success criteria
- QBR and business review cadence

## The critical rule

**Health scores are heuristics, never verdicts.** Never say an account "will
churn" based on a score. Surface the specific signals instead:

> "Usage dropped 30% over six weeks, no exec contact in 45 days, and there is a
> P1 open. That is a high-risk profile."

That is actionable. "Health is red" is not.

## Staleness

Data older than 14 days should be flagged as potentially stale. Always note when
information was last verified — a green band from five weeks ago tells you about
five weeks ago.

# Churn Signal Tiers

## Tier 1 — Structural Risk (at deal close)

Signals present at the point of sale that predict future churn:

- Excessive discounting
- Extended sales cycle
- Single-threaded deal — only one stakeholder
- Missing outcome catalog triggers
- No documented success criteria

These are inherited. You cannot fix the deal, but you can compensate — multi-
thread early and document success criteria that Sales did not.

## Tier 2 — Behavioral Risk (30–90 days post-onboarding)

Early adoption warning signs:

- Adoption stalling
- Outcome catalog progress stalled
- Champion departure
- Missed business reviews
- Low engagement with the CSM

Champion departure is a high-leverage Tier 2 signal — it invalidates your
MEDDPICC Champion element entirely and requires re-qualification from scratch.

## Tier 3 — Late-Stage Risk (90–120 days pre-renewal)

Renewal-window danger signals:

- Declining health trend over 60+ days
- Stalled renewal conversation
- Support spike
- Executive disengagement
- NPS at or below 6
- No exec contact in 45+ days (Enterprise)

**Tier 3 signals require a named escalation owner and explicit next steps.** Never
surface a Tier 3 flag without a path forward — an unowned risk flag is noise.

---

## Decision posture on escalation

A false positive costs one extra call. A false negative costs a surprise churn.
When genuinely uncertain, escalate earlier.
