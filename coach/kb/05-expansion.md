# Expansion

## The qualification gate

Expansion signals become pipeline only after **both**:

1. A conversation with the Economic Buyer
2. A formal opportunity created in CRM

Before both exist, it is an early signal — not pipeline. Tag it
`[early signal — not yet qualified]` and exclude it from NRR.

This gate is MEDDPICC's Economic Buyer plus Decision Process, applied to
expansion. Skipping it is a common cause of inflated expansion forecasts.

---

## When to deploy expansion motion

All of the following:

- Account health is green
- Active usage
- Champion relationship strong
- Renewal is 90–180 days away
- A QBR has surfaced documented success

That last one carries a lot of weight — expansion without documented Layer 2
value evidence is asking for more budget on the strength of a promise.

## When not to pursue expansion

Any of the following:

- Health is red or critical
- Account is in escalation or recovery
- Renewal is under 30 days away
- No AE assigned
- Adoption coverage below 60% without a documented override

Expanding into an unhealthy account converts a retention problem into a larger
retention problem.

---

## Expansion signal strength

**Strong**
- Feature adoption above 80%
- High seat utilization
- New use cases emerging
- New departments requesting access

**Moderate**
- Feature adoption 60–80%
- Multi-department interest
- Feature requests at scale

**Insufficient**
- Below 60% adoption
- No new use case signals
- Flat or declining usage

Insufficient signals are not a reason to push harder on expansion — they are a
reason to work adoption first.

---

## Qualifying the expansion (model note)

Once a signal is strong, qualify with MEDDPICC before forecasting. Elements that
often turn out hollow on expansion:

- **Metrics** — the customer needs their own model, not yours
- **Critical Event** (SPICED) — without one, expansion stalls indefinitely. This
  is a common reason expansion slips quarter to quarter.
- **Economic Buyer** — expansion frequently crosses into a budget the champion
  does not control

Use SPICED to find the new use case, MEDDPICC to qualify it, TARO to run the
play.
