# Onboarding

Time-to-value is the key metric for this stage. Everything below serves it.

## Milestone gates M1–M5

| Gate | Definition |
|---|---|
| **M1** | Kickoff completed, success criteria documented, stakeholders mapped |
| **M2** | Technical setup complete, initial users activated |
| **M3** | Core feature adoption achieved — first value milestone |
| **M4** | Expanded adoption, secondary use cases live |
| **M5** | Handoff to CSM complete, success plan active, time-to-value achieved |

M3 is especially important. An account that reaches M2 and stalls has a
working deployment nobody uses — technically live, practically churned.

---

## Risk signals in onboarding

- Missed kickoff
- Executive disengagement within 30 days
- Stalled at M1 or M2 with no documented blocker
- Missing decision-maker contact
- Time-to-value trending above your SLA cohort average

"Stalled with no documented blocker" is the important one. A stall with a known
blocker is a project-management problem. A stall without one often means the
customer has deprioritized you and has not said so.

## Escalation triggers

Escalate when any of these occur:

- Missed SLA milestone
- Technical blocker unresolved 5+ business days
- Executive sponsor departure
- CSM unable to get a response for 10+ business days

Each escalation needs a named owner and a next step — guardrail 4 applies here
as much as anywhere.

---

## Handoff quality

Onboarding inherits whatever Sales handed over. Score the handoff across five
dimensions — see the RevOps document for the full rubric. Passing threshold is
80/100.

Below threshold, you have two options: conditional pass with documented
remediation actions, or fail and require Sales to re-engage. Proceeding on a
failed handoff without remediation means you are building onboarding on
undocumented success criteria, which surfaces later as a Tier 1 churn signal you
cannot trace.

**Validate the SPICED that Sales captured.** If the Situation, Pain, and Impact
do not survive first contact with the customer, the deal was sold on a story the
buyer does not recognize — find out now, not at renewal.
