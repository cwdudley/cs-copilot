# Revenue Operations

## Forecast discipline

Pipeline coverage analysis requires a **segment-level** view — what is the
coverage ratio at each stage, per segment. Aggregate coverage hides the segment
that is actually failing.

Variance from plan needs a root cause, classified as one of:

- Rep-level pattern
- Deal-size cohort issue
- Seasonal factor
- Product or segment problem

**Three or more matching deals are required to classify something as systemic.**
Below that, it is anecdote. Resist the pull to build a program around two deals.

---

## GTM pulse metrics

Weekly view:

- Pipeline by stage
- Revenue run-rate vs. target
- GTM velocity — time-in-stage
- Churn signals by tier
- Cross-functional summary

**Leadership needs segment-level shifts and ARR at risk — not individual account
detail.** Rolling up account narratives into a leadership review is a common
way a pulse report becomes unreadable. Name the segment movement, then
offer account detail on request.

---

## Sales-to-CS handoff quality

Five dimensions, scored:

1. Outcome catalog entry complete
2. Trigger match documented
3. Measurement access established
4. Stakeholder map provided
5. Risk documentation present

**Passing threshold: 80/100.**

Below threshold:
- **Conditional pass** with documented remediation actions, or
- **Fail**, requiring Sales to re-engage

Handoff quality is an early, controllable predictor of Tier 1 structural
churn risk. A failed handoff that proceeds unremediated shows up 11 months later
as a renewal with no documented success criteria.

---

## Deal desk governance

**Discount approval SLAs:**
- Standard: 24 hours
- Final weeks of quarter: 4 hours

Non-standard terms require a named approver and documented rationale.

**Revenue leakage patterns to watch:**

- Excessive discount concentration — by rep, segment, or period
- Single-threaded high-ACV deals — a Tier 1 churn signal originating at deal desk
- Close date drift indicating sandbagging

Discount concentration and single-threading are worth flagging to CS, not just
Sales. Both predict post-sale risk, and CS inherits them without being told.
