# Cross-Framework Terminology Crosswalk

This document maps concepts across frameworks **without merging their terms**.
Use each framework's own term when speaking about that framework, and say which
framework a definition comes from.

Mapping strength:

- **Overlapping** — related concepts that are defined differently
- **Partial** — one framework's concept covers only part of the other's
- **None** — no equivalent element in the other framework

---

## Critical Event (Winning by Design) and Compelling Event (MEDDICC)

**Mapping: overlapping, with conflicting use of the phrase "compelling event."**

| Term | Framework and source | Definition (paraphrased) |
|---|---|---|
| **Critical Event** | SPICED — Winning by Design, current canonical page (`spiced/spiced-framework`) | The deadline, milestone or external force that creates urgency and determines when a decision must be made |
| **Critical Event** | SPICED — Winning by Design, 2022 research paper (`spiced/operating-model-for-recurring-revenue`) | A specific deadline by which the customer must achieve the desired impact or suffer negative consequences; it drives the purchase timeline |
| *compelling event* | Winning by Design, 2022 research paper | Explicitly contrasted with a Critical Event: a compelling event has **no** specific deadline |
| **Compelling Event** | MEDDICC (`meddpicc/compelling-event`) | The specific, **time-bound** reason a buyer must act — a date tied to a business, financial or regulatory consequence. Not a separate MEDDPICC letter; it runs through the other elements. |

**How to use:** In SPICED conversations say Critical Event; in MEDDPICC
conversations say Compelling Event. Don't carry Winning by Design's "compelling
event has no deadline" sense into MEDDICC material, or the reverse. Both
frameworks agree that urgency should be real and anchored to a consequence, not
invented by the seller.

## Pain

**Mapping: overlapping.**

| Term | Framework and source | Definition (paraphrased) |
|---|---|---|
| **Pain** | SPICED — Winning by Design (`spiced/spiced-framework`) | The specific friction, inefficiency or risk the customer experiences as a result of their situation |
| **Implicate the Pain** | MEDDPICC — MEDDICC, current (`meddpicc/implicate-the-pain`) | Identify the problem, indicate its wider consequences, and implicate the customer in those consequences |
| **Identify Pain** | MEDDIC — original letter; appears in historical material (`spiced/comparison-meddic-and-spiced-wbd-view`) | Superseded by Implicate the Pain in MEDDICC's current content |

**How to use:** "Identify Pain" is not the current MEDDPICC term. When an older
source says it, say it is older terminology.

## Impact and Metrics

**Mapping: overlapping.**

| Term | Framework and source | Definition (paraphrased) |
|---|---|---|
| **Impact** | SPICED — Winning by Design (`spiced/spiced-framework`, `spiced/emotional-vs-rational-impact`) | The consequences of solving or not solving the problem, in business terms, including both **rational** outcomes and **emotional** stakes |
| **Metrics** | MEDDPICC — MEDDICC (`meddpicc/metrics`) | Quantifiable measures of the value the solution provides, structured as M1s, M2s and M3s |

**How to use:** Winning by Design's Impact explicitly includes emotional impact;
MEDDICC's Metrics are quantified. Don't describe emotional impact as a MEDDPICC
Metric.

## Decision and its MEDDPICC counterparts

**Mapping: partial.** SPICED holds as one element what MEDDPICC splits apart.

| Term | Framework and source | Definition (paraphrased) |
|---|---|---|
| **Decision** | SPICED — Winning by Design (`spiced/spiced-framework`) | How the customer chooses: stakeholders, success criteria, trade-offs, and the process required to move forward |
| **Decision Criteria** | MEDDPICC — MEDDICC (`meddpicc/decision-criteria`) | The technical, economic and relationship criteria used to judge the purchase |
| **Decision Process** | MEDDPICC — MEDDICC (`meddpicc/decision-process`) | The steps the buyer follows to decide |
| **Paper Process** | MEDDPICC — MEDDICC (`meddpicc/paper-process`) | The steps from decision to signature: legal, security, procurement |
| **Economic Buyer** | MEDDPICC — MEDDICC (`meddpicc/economic-buyer`) | The person with overall authority in the buying decision |

## Value milestones

**Mapping: unresolved.** The public sources do not state whether these terms are
synonyms.

| Term | Framework and source | Definition (paraphrased) |
|---|---|---|
| **First Impact** | Winning by Design (`account-management/first-impact`) | A meaningful outcome the customer experiences — not a technical milestone. Onboarding is complete only when it is achieved and acknowledged. |
| **First Value / Full Value** | Winning by Design (`account-management/customer-success-for-impact`, `account-management/joint-impact-plan`) | First Value is the initial realization of value; Full Value is value realized across the complete use case |
| **Value vs. Impact** | Winning by Design (`expansion/bowtie-expansion-types`) | Value is a promise of future impact; Impact is the fulfillment of that promise |
| **First value milestone (M3)** | SuccessCOACHING (`coach/kb/06-onboarding`) | Core feature adoption achieved |

**How to use:** Given Winning by Design's own distinction between Value and
Impact, don't treat First Value and First Impact as interchangeable. Name the
source when using either term.

## M3 within MEDDICC

**Mapping: variance inside one framework.**

| Source | Definition (paraphrased) |
|---|---|
| `meddpicc/metrics` (canonical) | M2s validated after go-live — value actually realized |
| `account-management/meddic-for-customer-success-webinar` (practitioner) | A value goal that moves from proposal to commitment and becomes an M1 once achieved |

The canonical definition takes precedence for MEDDPICC.

## "Value" across frameworks

The same word carries different meanings:

| Sense | Framework and source |
|---|---|
| A promise of future impact | Winning by Design (`expansion/bowtie-expansion-types`) |
| A driver group: Implicate the Pain, Metrics, Decision Criteria | MEDDICC (`account-management/meddpicc-customer-lifecycle-intro`) |
| Value realization stages V1–V5 | SuccessCOACHING (`coach/kb/02-lifecycle-and-value`) |
