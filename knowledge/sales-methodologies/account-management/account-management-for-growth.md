# Account Management for Growth — Winning by Design's AM Curriculum

> This is a course outline, not the course content. Its value to the corpus is
> the **structure** Winning by Design uses to teach account management, and the
> learning objectives, which state what good AM practice looks like.

## Audience

CSMs and Account Managers responsible for renewals, expansion and account growth.

## Frameworks the course builds on

- Revenue Architecture and the **Bowtie**
- **SPICED** as the foundation of the Customer Success operating model
- **Strategic whitespace analysis**
- **Trigger plays**

## Curriculum and objectives (paraphrased)

### Week 1 — Operating model and discovery

**Module 1: Operating Model for Customer Success.**
Understand how every phase of the customer journey connects and influences
renewal and expansion outcomes, using SPICED for a holistic view of the account.

**Module 2: Uncovering expansion opportunities.**
Ask customer-focused questions throughout the journey to keep account insight
current, surface risks, and reveal expansion opportunities.

### Week 2 — Strategic planning for growth

**Module 3: Strategic planning and collaboration.**
Pull information from across the customer journey and from other customer-facing
roles to build a renewal and growth strategy.

**Module 4: Managing an effective renewal cycle.**
Earn the renewal by making sure the customer is aware of the impact already
achieved, understanding the new goals they want to reach, and showing why the
solution is indispensable. Includes objection handling.

### Week 3 — Expansion and relationships

**Module 5: Surface and secure expansions.**
Find upsell and cross-sell opportunities and use strategic whitespace analysis and
account planning to bring them to close. Includes objection handling.

**Module 6: Building executive relationships.**
Build strong executive relationships from the start, get past gatekeepers, and
deepen relationships with stakeholders and executives through every stage of the
partnership.

### Week 4 — Securing the deal

**Module 7: Trade vs. negotiate.**
Navigate renewal and expansion deals by anchoring on the customer's prioritized
goals, balancing give and get, and finding win-win outcomes.

**Module 8: Compelling storytelling.**
Use stories to build relationships and illustrate impact on the customer's
business, and move renewals and expansions to close without pitching features.

## The implied AM operating sequence

Read in order, the curriculum describes a sequence an AM coach can use:

1. Hold a **holistic, SPICED-based view** of the account
2. Keep insight **current** with ongoing customer-focused questions
3. Build a **renewal and growth strategy** with input from other roles
4. **Earn the renewal** through demonstrated impact
5. Use **whitespace analysis and account planning** to find and close expansion
6. Build **executive relationships** and multithread
7. **Trade, don't negotiate**, on renewal and expansion terms
8. **Tell impact stories** instead of pitching

The ordering is inferred from the published curriculum, not stated as a process
by Winning by Design.
