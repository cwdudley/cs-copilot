# Customer Success for Impact — Winning by Design's Post-Sale Curriculum

> Course outline only. Useful for the structure and vocabulary Winning by Design
> uses for recurring impact, executive business reviews and save plays.

## Aim

Deliver recurring value at every stage of the post-sale journey to drive
retention, expansion and recurring revenue.

## Key concepts

- **First Value** — the initial moment the customer realizes value
- **Full Value** — value realized across the customer's complete use case
- **Impact** as the organizing principle of Customer Success
- **SPICED applied post-sale** as a shared diagnostic language
- **Executive Business Reviews (EBRs)** to surface retention and expansion
- **Save Plays** — predefined responses triggered by risk events or concerning
  conversations

## Curriculum (paraphrased)

### Week 1 — The SaaS method for Customer Success
1. How selling has changed, and the critical moments in the customer journey,
   including First Value and Full Value
2. SPICED as a shared diagnostic language for customer success
3. Communication fundamentals: tone, active listening, opening a conversation

### Week 2 — Impact
4. Structuring executive customer calls: openings that earn attention and
   executive presence
5. Asking questions that help customers articulate the impact they want,
   including going deeper and handling unexpected answers
6. Orchestrating a plan that ties customer goals to timelines for reaching
   impact — "First Value and getting to impact"

### Week 3 — Storytelling and impact review
7. Running **Executive Business Reviews** with stakeholders across the journey to
   identify expansion opportunities
8. Using stories to influence customers and reveal new impact opportunities
   without pitching

### Week 4 — Expanding the partnership
9. Handling difficult conversations by balancing empathy with action
10. **Save Plays** triggered by risk events or worrying conversations
11. Engaging decision makers and getting past blockers to grow the partnership

## Account-management relevance (model note)

Supplies the vocabulary for three AM moments the corpus otherwise lacks detail on:
EBRs as an expansion-discovery mechanism, save plays for at-risk renewals, and
First Value / Full Value as the value milestones renewals rest on.
