# Customer Success Operating Model — Blueprint Summary

> **Partial source.** Winning by Design's full CS Operating Model is gated. The
> individually published chapter PDFs (Core Elements, Metrics, Onboarding,
> Expansion, Skills) were no longer available at retrieval.

## Structure

Customer Success operations organized around three lifecycle phases, inside the
Bowtie:

1. **Onboarding** — implementation and setup
2. **Adoption** — sustained usage and value realization
3. **Expansion** — growth in usage and revenue

The series also included chapters on core elements, metrics and core skills.

## Core principles

- **Recurring impact drives recurring revenue** — not one-time adoption, and not
  the renewal event itself.
- Customer Success needs a **formal operating model**, not an accumulation of ad
  hoc best practices.
- **First Impact marks the true end of onboarding** and predicts retention.
- **SPICED** provides the shared diagnostic language across teams.
- **Health scoring, trigger plays and business reviews** are the mechanisms for
  managing risk and opportunity proactively.
- **Expansion only succeeds when customers have achieved measurable emotional and
  functional impact.**
- The model gives Sales, CS and RevOps common language, metrics and
  interoperable workflows.

## Account-management relevance

Two principles here are load-bearing for an AM coach:

- Expansion is gated on **achieved impact**, not on usage or relationship
  strength.
- Renewal risk and expansion opportunity are managed through the **same**
  mechanisms: health signals, trigger plays and business reviews.
