# First Impact

> **Partial source.** Detailed plays are in the gated PDF.

## Definition

**A meaningful outcome the customer experiences — not a technical milestone like
a first login.** The moment the customer gets tangible value from the product or
service during onboarding.

## Onboarding is complete only when First Impact is achieved

Onboarding is not done when setup finishes or users log in. It is done when First
Impact has been reached **and acknowledged by the customer**.

## Why it matters

- **Shorter time to First Impact correlates strongly with higher retention.**
- **Losing momentum after onboarding raises churn risk**, even when First Impact
  was achieved.

## How to reach it

1. Define a specific, outcome-based First Impact during the **business kickoff**.
2. Align implementation work to reach it quickly.
3. Make it **visible, confirmed and time-bound**.
4. Get **explicit customer confirmation** that it was reached, which resets
   expectations and moves the relationship into its next phase.

## Account-management relevance

First Impact is the earliest point of evidence for a renewal case. An account
that never reached an acknowledged First Impact has no documented value to
renew on.
