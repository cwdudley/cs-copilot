# Joint Impact Plan

> **Partial source.** Step-by-step build instructions and examples are in the
> gated PDF.

## What it is

A collaborative, **living** document defining how the customer achieves value
over a **12-month** journey. It replaces vague success definitions with a
concrete, customer-facing roadmap tied to business impact and milestones.

## Components

- **Impact goals co-created with the customer** — not assumed by the vendor
- **Milestones and metrics** tied to outcomes
- A **12-month value-realization timeline**
- **Shared accountability** between vendor and customer, in writing
- A **First Value milestone** marking the real completion of onboarding

## Benefits

- Builds momentum and lowers churn risk
- Creates shared accountability through a written agreement
- Makes renewals more predictable because outcomes are agreed up front
- Reduces surprise churn when the plan extends past the renewal date
- Grounds expansion conversations in impact already achieved

## Intended for

CS leaders, CSMs, account managers and revenue leaders, especially teams moving
from adoption-based to impact-based customer success.

## Account-management relevance

Two details matter for renewals and expansion:

- Extending the plan **past the renewal date** means the customer is already
  planning a future with you when the renewal arrives.
- Expansion is framed as the **next set of impact goals**, not a new product
  pitch.

## Related concept

MEDDICC's Go-Live Plan (see `meddpicc/paper-process-go-live-plan.md`) is a
pre-sale mutual plan anchored on go-live. The Joint Impact Plan is the post-sale
counterpart anchored on realized impact.
