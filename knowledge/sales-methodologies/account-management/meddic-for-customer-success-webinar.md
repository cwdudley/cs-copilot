# MEDDIC for Customer Success — Webinar Takeaways

## Three post-sale imperatives

1. **Value** delivery
2. **Stakeholder** management
3. **Process** understanding

## Value

### With vs. without, not before vs. after

Frame value as the difference between having the solution and not having it,
rather than a one-time before-and-after comparison. It keeps the relevance
ongoing.

### Pain amnesia

Pain does not disappear at signature. But once the pain stops being felt, it gets
forgotten — even while the solution is actively solving it. That is how renewal
urgency quietly drains away. Different stakeholders also feel pain and gain
differently.

### Decision Criteria

Well-defined criteria give the post-sale team the rationale behind the purchase.
Teams that educate customers beyond technical specifications make a lasting
impression.

### Metrics — M1 / M2 / M3

| Type | Meaning | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| M1 | Proven value from the existing customer base | Fast, evidence-based education |
| M2 | A customer-specific use case | "Here is how we would do it for you" |
| M3 | A value goal evolved from an M2 | Moves from proposal to commitment; achieved M3s become M1s |

**Challenges:** customers who won't answer qualifying questions, data that is
unavailable, and stakeholders with competing priorities for their time.

## Stakeholders

- **Champions** — already identified during the sale; critical to renewal; they
  get things done.
- **Economic Buyers** — keep final veto and need sustained engagement. Worst case:
  they like you and still don't renew. Best case: they become a rainmaker for you.
- **Competition and detractors** — competition does not end at close. Two threats:
  internal detractors arguing against renewal, and competing vendors returning at
  renewal time.

## Renewal Decision Process

A renewal's decision process is structurally **different** from the original
purchase. Understand three things: the mechanics, the people, and the timing.

**The Paper Process is where renewals slip.**

## Five Customer Success plays

1. **Handoff excellence** — use MEDDPICC for the sales-to-CS transition so the
   customer isn't asked the same questions twice
2. **Value Pyramid** — keep the customer's strategic priorities visible after
   handover
3. **Success plan design** — structure the plan around Value, Stakeholders and
   Process
4. **Churn as competition** — treat renewal risk with the urgency of a competitive
   threat
5. **Decision Process blueprint** — use the renewal decision process as the map
   for engagement

## Failure modes

| Risk | Mechanism | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Pain amnesia | Solution works → pain fades → urgency lost | Renewal vulnerability |
| Stakeholder drift | Losing EB or Champion engagement | Lost influence and advocacy |
| Metrics avoidance | No value baseline established | No quantified renewal case |
| Process blindness | Renewal mechanics never learned | Surprised by process changes |
| Paper Process mismanagement | Administrative delay | Renewals slip through the cracks |
