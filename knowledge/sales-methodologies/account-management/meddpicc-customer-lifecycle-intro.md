# MEDDPICC as a Full Customer Lifecycle Framework — Introduction

## Core idea

MEDDPICC as a shared language across the whole go-to-market organization, not
only a sales qualification tool.

## The three drivers the elements group into

| Driver | Elements |
|---|---|
| **Value** | Implicate the Pain, Metrics, Decision Criteria |
| **Stakeholders** | Champion, Economic Buyer, Competition |
| **Process** | Decision Process, Paper Process |

This Value / Stakeholders / Process grouping recurs across MEDDICC's material and
is a compact way to inspect any account or opportunity.

## Lifecycle stages

Awareness, consideration, decision, and post-sale / expansion — with different
teams engaging at each.

## Roles covered by the series

Account Executives, SDRs, Solutions Engineers, Partners, Marketing, Product,
Customer Success, and RevOps.

## Key principle

Different people value the same solution differently. The same pain produces
distinct value perspectives depending on the role — a CMO's view and a CFO's view
are not the same.

## Why a single language

Historically, teams used different frameworks — SDRs with BANT, CS with its own
platform concepts — which fragmented context. A shared language lets the
organization coordinate.
