# MEDDPICC for Customer Success

MEDDICC's position: MEDDPICC continues past the signature, giving Sales and
post-sale teams a shared language through handoff, value realization, renewal and
expansion.

## What each element hands over to Customer Success

| Element | Post-sale meaning |
|---|---|
| **Metrics** | The metrics used to prove potential value become the **M3** KPIs Customer Success works toward |
| **Economic Buyer** | Sales identifies or strongly indicates them; CS determines which executives to stay aligned with. Critical for renewal and expansion. |
| **Decision Criteria** | CS learns the technical, economic and relationship basis of the purchase, which informs how it keeps proving superiority |
| **Decision Process** | Predicts how efficiently and when renewals and upsells will happen — especially valuable in subscription businesses |
| **Paper Process** | CS learns the agreement requirements and timelines, so renewal and expansion contracting is not a surprise |
| **Implicated Pain** | CS understands the specific problems the solution was bought to solve, which guides value delivery |
| **Champion** | A clearly identified contact carries engagement beyond the deal |
| **Competition** | CS knows which alternatives the customer considered and may still favor, which shapes renewal differentiation |

## CS applications by stage

**Value stage**
- Turn M3s into realized outcomes
- Differentiate through demonstrated value
- Influence future Decision Criteria through delivery

**Growth and retention stage**
- Use new **M1s** to expand into additional use cases or modules
- Use knowledge of the Decision and Paper Processes to make renewals and upsells
  predictable
- Work with Marketing to turn achieved M3s into M1s for future prospects

**Identifying expansion**
- Quantify the right value for the account
- Engage the right level of stakeholder
- Align with the Paper and Decision Processes well before renewal

## Principle

Customer Success's scope runs from signature to complete value delivery, and CS
managers use MEDDPICC when identifying expansion opportunities.
