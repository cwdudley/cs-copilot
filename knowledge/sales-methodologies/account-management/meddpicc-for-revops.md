# MEDDPICC for RevOps — Pressure-Testing Deal Quality

## Role

RevOps uses MEDDPICC with the CRM as a common language from selling through
retention. In closing and implementation it captures deal information for clean
handoffs and forecasting. In the value, growth and retention stages it works with
Customer Success to monitor value delivery.

## Eight pressure-test questions

| Element | Question |
|---|---|
| **Metrics** | Do our teams agree on the measurable value this solution delivers? |
| **Economic Buyer** | Has the decision authority been identified and engaged? |
| **Decision Criteria** | Are we actively shaping the criteria or reacting to them? |
| **Decision Process** | Can we see the decision risks and where the date could slip? |
| **Paper Process** | What contracting or approval obstacles remain? |
| **Pain** | Is the pain consistently identified and revisited across stakeholders? |
| **Champion** | Is the Champion qualified, with vested interest verified? |
| **Competition** | Have both competitors and inertia been analyzed? |

## Use

These questions remove subjectivity from deal-quality assessment and apply
equally to renewal and expansion opportunities in the forecast.
