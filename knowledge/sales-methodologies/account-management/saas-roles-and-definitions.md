# Post-Sale Roles and Definitions

## Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| **Account Manager (AM)** | Joins after the customer has committed. Owns the **commercial** side of the relationship and works with customer success to identify expansion and other contract opportunities. Companies create AM teams so new-business sellers can focus on new logos. |
| **Customer Success Manager (CSM)** | Proactively drives **adoption and net retention** and ensures customers reach expected outcomes. Early on, often covers onboarding, QBRs, support, training and renewals; shifts toward strategic customer conversations as the company scales. Builds relationships; not responsible for technical work or support. |
| **Onboarding Manager (ONB)** | Drives initial deployment and the early key moments of the journey, so the customer sees value early. |
| **Technical Account Manager (TAM)** | Delivers technical services such as custom integrations and migrations; combines technical and customer skills. |

## How roles separate as companies grow

Winning by Design describes Customer Success getting overwhelmed by onboarding
work at around $3–4M ARR, and separate Onboarding and Customer Success roles
emerging at around $5–8M ARR. The point it makes: departments must operate as one
system, not silos.

The source does not formally assign ownership of upsell, cross-sell, renewal or
resell, beyond placing expansion with the AM and adoption and retention with the
CSM.

## Definitions

- **Churn** — the rate at which customers stop doing business with you over a
  period
- **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)** — the estimated total revenue an account
  generates over its life as a customer
- **Recurring revenue** — customers buy on a recurring basis rather than once
- **Upsell** — getting a customer to spend more by buying an upgraded or premium
  version of what they have
- **Onboarding** — integrating a new customer with the product or service after
  purchase

Cross-sell and resell are defined in the Bowtie source in the `expansion` folder.
