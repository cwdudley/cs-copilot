# Mapping Relationships

> **Partial source.** The four plays, templates and examples are in the gated
> PDF.

## Core ideas

- Complex deals rarely close on a single relationship.
- **Mapping influence reveals risk that pipeline stages cannot show.** A deal can
  look healthy by stage and still rest on one person.

## What the blueprint covers

- **Four plays for reaching decision makers**
- **Multithreading** to reduce dependency on any single contact or role
- **Organizational structure mapping**
- **Sentiment analysis** to prioritize which relationships to invest in
- **Decision-flow choreography** — sequencing who engages whom, and when

## Intended for

Sales leaders, AEs, enterprise teams, and go-to-market teams running
multi-stakeholder deals **and expansion motions**. Winning by Design notes that
teams running cross-sell, upsell and re-sell motions benefit from reducing
single-contact dependency.

## Account-management relevance

For an AM, the relationship map is a standing account artifact, not a deal
artifact. Coverage gaps, negative sentiment and single-threaded relationships are
renewal risks before they are expansion blockers.
