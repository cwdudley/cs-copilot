# What Multi-Threading Really Means

## Quality over contact count

The common rule — get at least five contacts — misses the point. Whyte would take
engagement with **one Economic Buyer and one qualified Champion** over many
coaches with no influence. What matters is stakeholder quality and depth of
engagement, not headcount.

## The overlooked half: your own side

Most teams think only about threading into the customer. Real multi-threading
also means **your** organization is engaged at matching levels:

- Your senior executives connected to their Economic Buyers
- Your sales manager engaged with an Economic Buyer and the Champion
- Your sales engineer connected to their technical buyers

## Three risks of single-threading on your side

1. **Fragility** — if the one seller is unavailable (leaves, is ill, on leave), the
   deal collapses
2. **Bias** — multiple internal perspectives counter optimistic "happy ears" and
   triangulate the truth
3. **Value** — the customer gets broader support and more perspectives from your
   organization

## Audit questions

- How qualified are our key customer stakeholders?
- Who on our team, beyond the account owner, is actively engaged?

## Account-management relevance

Relationship fragility is a renewal risk as much as a deal risk. An account held
together by a single AM-to-Champion relationship is exposed when either person
changes roles.
