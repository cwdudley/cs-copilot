# The Stakeholder Meeting

> **Partial source.** Detailed agenda and facilitation guidance are in the gated
> PDF.

## Purpose

Run multi-executive meetings that produce alignment, commitment and forward
motion — moving stakeholders from a shared understanding of the current state to
agreement on impact, priorities and next steps.

## Three-phase design

1. **Current-state alignment** — anchor on shared metrics and the as-is situation
   to establish credibility
2. **Future-state impact** — co-create the possible outcomes with the executives
3. **Commitment and action** — agree priorities, timelines and clear owners

## Principles

- **Design for alignment, not presentation.** Whiteboarding beats slides because
  it invites collaboration and reduces post-meeting stall.
- **Shared metrics often become the basis of the decision criteria.**
- **Explicit prioritization** prevents momentum loss after the meeting.
- **Co-creation increases executive engagement and ownership.**

## Account-management relevance (model note)

This structure maps cleanly onto an executive business review aimed at
expansion: confirm the impact achieved (current state), co-create the next impact
goals (future state), and leave with prioritized, owned next steps.
