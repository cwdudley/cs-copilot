# Expansion & Growth and Impact & Retention Playbooks

> Service descriptions. The substance is in the **named components**, which
> define what a systematic expansion and retention motion includes.

## Expansion & Growth Playbook

**Problem:** expansion depends on whichever account manager happens to spot an
opening, instead of a system.

**Components:**
- **Whitespace analysis**
- **Account planning**
- **Trigger plays** that surface expansion at the right moment
- **One motion shared by CS, AM and Sales**
- Net revenue retention built on structure rather than luck

**Example deliverable:** an expansion journey map.

## Impact & Retention Playbook

**Problem:** renewals get risky when value goes unmanaged between onboarding and
the renewal date.

**Components:**
- A **proactive cadence of milestones**
- **Business reviews**
- **Renewal steps with clear ownership**
- **Early risk surfacing**
- **Continuous proof of impact** through the year, so renewals are earned across
  the year rather than chased at the end

**Example deliverable:** documented impact process.

## Account-management relevance

Together these name the parts of a complete AM system: whitespace analysis and
account planning to decide **where** to grow; trigger plays to decide **when**;
milestones and business reviews to keep **impact** visible; and owned renewal
steps so renewal is **earned, not chased**.
