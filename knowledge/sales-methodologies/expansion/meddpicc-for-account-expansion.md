# MEDDPICC Across the Lifecycle — Including Account Expansion

## Premise

Two limiting assumptions: that MEDDPICC is only for AEs, and that it only applies
to selling and closing. MEDDICC argues it applies across the whole lifecycle,
including existing accounts.

## By stage

**Awareness and prospecting**
- Who is the likely Champion, and who should be contacted?
- What pain areas can be surfaced?
- What value can be quantified?
- Which Decision Criteria should shape the outreach?

**Selling and closing**
- **Value:** implicate the pain, find quantifiable metrics, learn the criteria
- **Stakeholders:** confirm Champion and Economic Buyer; map Competition broadly —
  anyone or anything competing for the same resources
- **Process:** keep Decision Process and Paper Process distinct so the deal keeps
  moving

**Value and expansion**
- Measure delivery with consistent metrics that can be reused later
- Anticipate the Decision Criteria for upsells and cross-sells
- Identify use cases in new business units or territories

## Account expansion — the four questions

1. **Champion continuity** — will the Champion advocate for the next expansion?
2. **Economic Buyer** — who has veto power over expanding this account?
3. **Decision Process** — how is an expansion approved?
4. **Paper Process** — what would make executing the expansion easier?

## Urgency

MEDDICC's position is that sellers, not customers, control the timeline. The
value elements — Pain, Metrics, Decision Criteria — quantify the cost of inaction
and the gain from acting, creating urgency through evidence rather than pressure.

## Account-management relevance (model note)

Question 2 deserves particular attention in expansion: the Economic Buyer for a new use case or
business unit is often **not** the Economic Buyer who approved the original
purchase.
