# Rethinking Negotiation — MEDDICC's View

## The customer is not an adversary

MEDDICC rejects adversarial negotiation frameworks. Whyte contrasts selling with
hostage negotiation: the customer is a partner, not an opponent.

## Value beats tactics

Negotiation techniques, including popular hostage-negotiation methods, matter
less than a **strong, value-rich business case that the Champion distributes and
the Economic Buyer sponsors**. Techniques like mirroring still have a place in
discovery.

## Roles

- **Champion** — works with the seller to navigate procurement constructively
- **Economic Buyer** — sponsors the value case and gives it legitimacy
- **Procurement** — falls back on price comparison when value is unclear. Without
  differentiation, the only value comparison left is a cheaper competitor's price.

## Common mistakes

- Treating customers as adversaries
- Failing to articulate value, so procurement reduces the decision to price
- Relying on negotiation psychology instead of a business case
- Neglecting Economic Buyer sponsorship and Champion advocacy

## The strongest lever

A value-based business case with a differentiated solution and a real Compelling
Event.
