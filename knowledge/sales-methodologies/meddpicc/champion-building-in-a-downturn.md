# Building Champions in a Downturn

## FOMU replaces FOMO

Richard Dufty describes buying environments dominated by **fear of messing up**
rather than fear of missing out. Advocating for a vendor carries more personal
risk for the buyer, so Champions are harder to build.

Dufty reports that top performers who work through Champions are about four
times more successful than those who do not.

## Tactics

**Slow down discovery.** Understand the company-level pain in depth; this is also
how viable Champions are identified early.

**Use referrals.** Introductions from existing customers tend to reach peer-level
contacts who are more open to advocating.

**Build the narrative with them.** Help the Champion combine the technical view
with business outcomes for executive conversations.

**Align to the CFO.** Nick Reva's view: the Economic Buyer is now the CFO. Show
ROI against the specific metrics the Champion wants to improve, backed by proven
results.

**Reduce the Champion's personal risk.** Bundle professional services,
warranties, or proof-of-value elements so advocating for you is safer.

**Apply MEDDPICC continuously** — before, during and after proof of value,
through to go-live.

## Account-management relevance

The risk-reduction and referral tactics apply to expansions, where the Champion
is staking credibility already earned on the first purchase.
