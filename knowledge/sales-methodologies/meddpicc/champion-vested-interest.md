# Vested Interest — The Overlooked Champion Trait

## Core idea

A Champion without vested interest is just a contact. Nobody sells internally on
your behalf without a reason. Understanding what they personally gain when you
win is what activates real advocacy — and finding that reason is the work.

## Two forms of vested interest

1. **Personal / brand** — advancement, visibility, professional reputation
2. **Business** — the success of their organization or department

## Examples (Andy Whyte)

- A Champion at a startup wanted to raise their personal profile, and responded to
  opportunities like podcasts and live events.
- A long-tenured company loyalist cared only about the business succeeding;
  personal visibility meant nothing to them.

Same role, opposite motivations. Offering the wrong one does not activate either.

A third example: an individual described as a repeat Champion across employers,
consistently motivated by helping teams improve.

## How to discover it

Tell a story about a similar Champion and what they got out of it, then watch
whether it resonates. People reveal their own motivation in how they react to
someone else's.

## Coaching questions this supports

- What does your Champion personally win if this succeeds?
- Is their interest personal, business, or both — and how do you know?
