# Coaching With MEDDIC — The Three Ts

Order matters: tactics only land after trust and timing are in place.

## 1. Trust

Without trust, people will not bring you their problems. The article uses the
idea of a **trust battery**: new team members start with it charged, and managers
recharge it through vulnerability and transparency.

## 2. Timing

Coaching works best frequently and close to the problem. High-performing teams
address issues as they come up in daily work instead of saving them for the
weekly one-on-one.

## 3. Tactics

Methodology, product knowledge and craft. A manager strong on tactics but weak on
trust and timing will find their coaching falls flat.

## Coaching vs. telling

Real coaching is not instructions like "do this to overcome that objection." It
builds the psychological safety that lets people surface gaps and blockers
instead of hiding them.

## Gaps in this source

No example dialogue, question bank or cadence guidance beyond addressing issues
as they arise.
