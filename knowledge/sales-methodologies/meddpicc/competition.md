# Competition

## Definition

Any person, vendor or initiative competing for the same funds or resources you
are.

## Four types

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Rivals** | Direct vendor competitors |
| **Self-build** | The customer builds the capability internally |
| **Other initiatives** | Other projects or priorities competing for the same budget |
| **Inertia** | The status quo — choosing to do nothing |

Each needs a different strategy.

## Principles

- **Don't knock the competition.** Criticizing competitors does not work.
- **Set criteria early** so they favor your strengths and trap competitors.
- **Research thoroughly and use your Champion** to understand the landscape.

## Why it matters

Without understanding competitive forces, forecasts are unreliable. Competition
needs ongoing monitoring, not a one-time check.

You cannot win every deal, but you are responsible for being as prepared as
possible.
