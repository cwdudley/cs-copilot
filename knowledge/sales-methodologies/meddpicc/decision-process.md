# Decision Process

## Definition

The series of steps the buyer follows to make a decision.

## Two components

1. **Technical validation** — evaluating whether the solution works
2. **Business approval** — financial and strategic authorization

## Decision Process vs. Paper Process

- **Decision Process:** how the buyer decides
- **Paper Process:** how you get from that decision to a signature

## Principles

**Advance it proactively.** MEDDICC cites about 60% of deals being lost to
inertia. Deals have to be driven through each step rather than kept "warm."

**Engagement is not progress.** Demos, reference calls and reports feel like
movement. Without explicit confirmation that the buyer has moved to the next
step, nothing has advanced.

**Process length sets the timeline.** Time to close tracks the length of the
Decision Process; shortening the sequence improves velocity.

## Mapping

- Identify the stages early
- Track progress against them consistently
- Watch for changes in requirements or sequence
- Keep the relevant stakeholders involved throughout

## Common mistakes

- Treating activity volume as progression
- Not uncovering the process details early
- Not updating the map as the buyer's process changes
- Being passive and letting the deal stall
