# Five Things Not to Do With the Economic Buyer

| Don't | Why | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Assume anything** | Sellers frequently misjudge who the EB is or what they prioritize | Validate identity and priorities through the Champion first, so you arrive with an informed point of view |
| **2. Talk technical decision criteria** | EBs care about economic criteria; their teams own the technical detail | Lead with economic value and business outcomes |
| **3. Skip preparation** | Unprepared meetings burn credibility and a scarce opportunity | Research deeply; consult stakeholders, especially the Champion |
| **4. Go in cold** | Early access without a strategy wastes both sides' time | Engage when you have a strong point of view; coordinate the approach with the Champion |
| **5. Challenge blindly** | A challenge without credibility backfires | Earn credibility through preparation and relationships before introducing a challenging perspective |

## Coaching questions this supports (model note)

- What has the Champion confirmed about this Economic Buyer's priorities?
- What is your point of view for this meeting, in one sentence?
- Which economic criterion will you anchor on?
