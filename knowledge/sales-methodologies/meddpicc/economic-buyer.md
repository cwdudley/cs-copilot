# Economic Buyer

## Definition

The person with overall authority in the buying decision: able to push a
decision through when others object, and to stop it when others support it.

## Identification markers

- **Veto power** — can advance or halt the initiative regardless of other
  stakeholders
- Priorities tied to **strategic organizational objectives**
- **P&L responsibility**
- Access to **discretionary funds** outside standard budgets

Economic Buyers rarely announce themselves. They have to be actively identified.

## Relationship to the Champion

- The Champion is central to identifying and introducing the Economic Buyer.
- A Champion who cannot or will not arrange that introduction may not be a real
  Champion — treat it as a signal that more relationship work is needed.
- Champion cooperation on EB access is itself an indicator of deal health.

## Engaging the Economic Buyer

- Speak in their language: **economic** decision criteria and business
  outcomes, not features.
- They typically weigh **cost**, **time to value**, and **their team's
  confidence** in the initiative.

## Why it matters

The Economic Buyer has the largest influence on the outcome — both the deal size
and whether the deal happens at all.
