# Practitioner Panel on the MEDDPICC Elements

Panel: Em Radkowski (GTM operations leader), Caroline Woussen-Franczia (founder
and author), Cliff Dorsey (long-time sales leader and former CRO).

## Most critical element

- **Champion** (Woussen-Franczia) — and there is no Champion without pain.
- **Pain** (Radkowski) — must be clearly implicated before a deal progresses.
- **Metrics** (Dorsey) — they quantify the pain and create a cycle that generates
  urgency; top performers invest in defining the metrics that actually matter.

## Most underrated

**Decision Criteria.** Woussen-Franczia treats it as a capability requirement
from the start of discovery; it is often confused with price when it should be a
central differentiator. Radkowski notes sellers skim past it in transformational
technology deals.

**Metrics** (Radkowski) — sellers who get into detail with Champions and Economic
Buyers stand out; most do not spend enough time here.

## Can you create pain?

Woussen-Franczia: no — you can only uncover it, through:

- Discovery questions that do not feel like an interrogation
- Preparation on the industry, business and strategy
- Helping customers see problems they had not recognized — which is where
  implication comes in

## Adoption

- **Make it the common language**, embed it in the sales process, inspect it,
  and start at pipeline generation rather than two weeks before close (Dorsey).
- **Communication tool, not inspection checklist** — reps use it because it
  makes them more money, not because they are told to (Radkowski).
- **Executive buy-in** — frame it as the science of selling, which resonates with
  product and engineering-minded leaders (Dorsey).
- **Start where the business leaks** — introduce it where deals are being lost
  (Radkowski).

## Shared conclusion

MEDDPICC works best once it stops being a checklist and becomes a language.
