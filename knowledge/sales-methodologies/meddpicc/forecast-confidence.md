# Forecast Confidence

## Three root causes of inaccurate forecasts

1. **Poor qualification**
2. **Weak processes**
3. **Lack of trust**

Consequences: late-quarter slippage, lower profitability, and lost confidence
from stakeholders.

## How each shows up in MEDDPICC terms

| Root cause | Typical symptom |
|---|---|
| Qualification gaps | Coaches counted as Champions |
| Process failures | Reactive rather than proactive on the Paper Process |
| Trust deficits | Weaknesses concealed instead of surfaced |

## Reported result

MEDDICC cites a Series E marketing-technology company with four consecutive
quarters of forecast misses reaching over 98% forecast accuracy after
implementation. This is a vendor-reported case.

## Key idea

Forecast accuracy is a **leading indicator** of a healthy sales process, not just
a reporting output.

## Coaching questions this supports (model note)

- For each commit deal: is the Champion tested, and is the Paper Process mapped?
- What weakness in this forecast has not been said out loud yet?
