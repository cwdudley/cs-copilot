# Implicate the Pain

## Definition

The "I" in MEDDPICC. Not merely recognizing that a customer has a problem, but
helping them understand its wider consequences and take ownership of solving it.

## The three-stage progression

1. **Identify** — recognize and name the specific problem
2. **Indicate** — show the wider business effects and knock-on consequences
3. **Implicate** — connect the customer directly to those consequences, so they
   become accountable stakeholders in fixing it

## Why implication matters

If the customer does not truly feel the consequences, there is no urgency to
solve the pain or to move the deal forward. Solutions without attached urgency
get deprioritized behind initiatives that feel more critical.

Implication is what produces a credible answer to **why now?**

## Connections

- **Metrics** quantify the value relative to the pain.
- **Economic Buyer** must personally feel implicated to authorize the purchase.
- **Decision Criteria** are shaped by how the pain is implicated.
