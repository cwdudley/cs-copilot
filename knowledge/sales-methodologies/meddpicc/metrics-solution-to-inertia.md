# Metrics as a Solution to Inertia

## The problem

Roelofsen identifies **inertia — the no-decision outcome** — as the main reason
deals are lost. Not losing to a competitor: the customer simply does not act.

A root cause: most customer-side personas are not technology buyers by trade.
Asked to build a case and evaluate options, champions struggle because it is not
part of their job, and the deal stalls.

## How metrics overcome inertia

Three connected moves:

1. Find **significant pain** — worth acting on.
2. **Quantify the value** the customer can realistically achieve.
3. **Align to strategic business initiatives** — which gives the champion the
   backing to push internally.

The M1–M2–M3 framework links these across the whole customer lifecycle and gives
every go-to-market team the same value thread.

## Reactive vs. proactive

| Reactive | Proactive |
|---|---|
| Asking the customer to supply metrics | Co-discovering metrics with them |
| Isolated selling motions | A unified GTM approach |
| Professional seller | Trusted advisor |

## Coaching questions this supports

- If this deal dies, is the likeliest cause a competitor or no decision?
- Has the champion been given a quantified case they can defend without you?
- Which strategic initiative does this connect to?
