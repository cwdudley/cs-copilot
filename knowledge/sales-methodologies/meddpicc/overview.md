# MEDDPICC — Methodology Overview

## What it is

A qualification framework and methodology for complex B2B sales. MEDDICC
positions it as a shared language for judging deal health, finding gaps early,
and forecasting with more confidence.

## Lineage

- **MEDDIC** — created in 1996 at PTC; MEDDICC credits Dick Dunkel.
- **MEDDICC** — adds **Competition**.
- **MEDDPICC** — adds **Paper Process**; aimed at enterprise and mid-market
  deals where legal and procurement are in the loop.
- MEDDICC reports that a 2026 US federal court ruling treated MEDDPICC as a
  generic sales methodology term.

## The eight elements (first-party definitions, paraphrased)

| Element | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Metrics** | Quantifiable measures of the value the solution provides, structured as M1s, M2s, M3s |
| **Economic Buyer** | The person with overall authority in the buying decision — can approve when others object and block when others approve |
| **Decision Criteria** | The principles, guidelines and requirements used to judge the purchase: technical, economic, relationship |
| **Decision Process** | The steps the buyer follows to decide whether to buy |
| **Paper Process** | The steps after the decision that take you from decision to signature |
| **Implicate the Pain** | Not just identifying a problem, but indicating its wider effects and implicating the customer in its consequences |
| **Champion** | A person with power, influence and credibility inside the customer who is willing and able to help progress the deal |
| **Competition** | Any person, vendor or initiative competing for the same funds or resources |

## Terminology note

MEDDICC's first-party wording is **Implicate the Pain**, not "Identify Pain."
The element describes a progression: Identify → Indicate → Implicate. Older
MEDDIC material uses "Identify Pain."

## Variant fit (per MEDDICC)

| Variant | Best suited to |
|---|---|
| MEDDIC | Simpler B2B with fewer stakeholders |
| MEDDICC | Competitive markets needing differentiation |
| MEDDPICC | Enterprise/mid-market deals with formal legal and procurement |

## Before vs. after adoption (per MEDDICC)

**Without:** no visibility into why deals are won or lost; success hard to
repeat; next steps unclear; communication reactive.

**With:** faster qualification; transparent next steps; forecasting grounded
in consistent evidence.
