# The Go-Live Plan

## What it is

MEDDICC's form of a **mutual action plan** (also called a mutual close plan).
Unlike a seller-centric close plan, it is a shared plan between buyer and seller.

## The key difference

The goal is **the solution going live**, not the deal closing. The plan works
backward from the date the customer starts getting business impact.

## Components

1. **Customer impact focus** — a realistic timeline to when the customer sees
   value, which builds commitment beyond signature
2. **Deal closure integration** — ties the Paper Process to the go-live date, so
   urgency comes from a real delivery date instead of an arbitrary close date
3. **Mutual accountability** — aligns both sides' priorities, which also improves
   adoption and retention

## Why it works

Anchoring on go-live creates legitimate urgency (the customer wants the impact by
a date) and keeps the seller's team focused on value delivery rather than
signature.

## Account-management relevance (model note)

The same logic applies to renewals and expansions: plan backward from when the
customer needs the expanded capability live, or from the renewal date, and put
the Paper Process steps on that timeline.
