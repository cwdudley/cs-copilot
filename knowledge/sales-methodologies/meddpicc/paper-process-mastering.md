# Mastering the Paper Process

The administrative and contractual side of closing — argued to be among the most
consequential stages of the cycle.

## Five components

1. **Proposal creation** — tailored to the customer's specific needs and pain,
   reinforcing your position
2. **Quote generation** — accurate, timely, matching requirements
3. **Contract management** — drafting, negotiating and finalizing with a clear
   grasp of terms
4. **Compliance and legal requirements** — working with legal early to avoid
   late regulatory roadblocks
5. **Communication and collaboration** — keeping every stakeholder informed so
   issues surface and resolve quickly

## Operational practices

- Standardize reusable proposal, quote and contract templates
- Define approval paths and communication channels
- Train sellers on pricing, contract terms and compliance
- Use CRM, document management and e-signature tooling
- Collect feedback and refine continuously

## Why it pays off

Less administrative load on sellers, shorter cycles, a more professional customer
experience, fewer errors, and higher close rates. MEDDICC cites a survey finding
that 59% of sales leaders feel the agreement-and-signing stage takes too long.

## Coaching questions this supports (model note)

- Who, by name, is involved in legal, security and procurement?
- How long did those steps take last time, and are they already scheduled?
- What documents or questionnaires can be sent now, in parallel?
