# Paper Process

## Definition

The series of steps that follow the Decision Process: how the deal goes from a
decision to a signature.

## Three core elements

1. **Process** — the formal procedures and workflows
2. **People** — who has to approve
3. **Timing** — how long each step realistically takes

## Where deals typically get stuck

- Legal review
- Security sign-off
- Vendor questionnaires
- Master Service Agreement negotiation

## Why it is its own element

Even after the Champion and Economic Buyer have agreed to buy, a deal can stall
in the administrative phase. Separating Paper Process from Decision Process keeps
sellers pushing momentum through to signature and surfaces fixable issues early.

Staying in control of the Paper Process sharply reduces deal risk, and accurate
forecasting requires understanding it.

## Common pitfall

Treating "yes" as closed, and not actively managing the administrative workflow
afterward.
