# Nobody Regrets Qualifying Out

## Principle

Deciding early that an opportunity is not viable prevents wasted effort. MEDDICC
presents qualifying out as something no one regrets afterward.

## Two good outcomes

1. **A clear no.** The customer confirms it is not a priority, and your time goes
   to viable opportunities. A no is the second-best answer.
2. **Re-engagement.** The attempt to close the opportunity out can prompt the
   customer to re-prioritize and revive it.

## Signals to assess

- Does the contact have purchasing authority?
- Is there significant pain your solution addresses?
- How high a priority is the problem to them, really?

## Example

Adan Lewis used a light-hearted qualifying-out email (a voice-assistant GIF
"moving the deal to closed-lost") that drew positive replies because it was
original and its intent was clear.

## Account-management relevance (model note)

The same discipline applies to expansion hypotheses: an expansion idea with no
pain, no authority and no priority is better closed than carried in a forecast.
