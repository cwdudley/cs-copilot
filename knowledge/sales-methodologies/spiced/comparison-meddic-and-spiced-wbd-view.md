# MEDDIC and SPICED — Winning by Design's View

> **Source bias:** this is the SPICED creator's comparison. MEDDICC's
> counterpart is preserved separately in
> `meddpicc/comparison-spiced-vs-meddpicc-meddicc-view.md`.

## The two frameworks as described here

**MEDDIC** (1996, built for perpetual software): Metrics, Economic Buyer, Decision
Criteria, Decision Process, Identify Pain, Champion.

**SPICED** (2012, built by Winning by Design for SaaS): Situation, Pain, Impact
(emotional and rational), Critical Event, Decision.

## Differences as Winning by Design frames them

| Aspect | SPICED | MEDDIC |
|---|---|---|
| Orientation | Customer-centric, action-driven | Seller-centric, direction-driven |
| Lifecycle | Full customer journey | Sales funnel |
| Forecasting | Action-based | Stage-based |
| Functions | Sales, marketing, success, product | Primarily sales |
| Seller experience assumed | Accessible to sellers with a few years' experience | Suited to very experienced sellers |
| Emotional factor | Explicit emotional impact | Data-focused |
| Motions | Dedicated, high, mid, low and no-touch | Mainly high-touch enterprise |

## Making them interoperable

The article recommends that organizations keep their MEDDIC investment and
integrate SPICED rather than replace one with the other:

1. **Impact diagnosis** — use SPICED to uncover rational *and* emotional impact,
   enriching MEDDIC's Metrics.
2. **Decision Criteria** — build the identified impact into the decision criteria,
   giving stakeholders a quantitative frame.
3. **Decision Process** — combine MEDDIC's decision-maker focus with SPICED's
   buying-center roles. A useful formulation from the article: a champion is
   triggered by the impact, and a decider by the date they need that impact.

## Winning by Design's claim

All elements of MEDDIC can be found within SPICED, with SPICED adding elements for
broader applicability.

## Note on the two comparisons

The two vendors disagree on which framework contains the other. For the AM
Copilot, the useful takeaway both articles support is that the frameworks overlap
heavily and can be used together; each document preserves its own vendor's
claims.
