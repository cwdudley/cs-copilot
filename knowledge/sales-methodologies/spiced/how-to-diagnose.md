# How to Diagnose — Blueprint Summary

> **Partial source.** Example questions and the detailed conversation structure
> are in the gated PDF.

## Core principle

**Diagnosis must come before prescription.** Proposing a solution before
understanding the problem costs credibility and buyer trust.

## Diagnostic flow

1. **Prepare** for the conversation
2. **Open with ACE** — Winning by Design's opening structure to set clarity at the
   start (the acronym is expanded in the gated material)
3. **Probe Situation and Pain** with contextual questions
4. **Explore Impact, Critical Events and the Decision process** — the urgency and
   the drivers
5. **Summarize with empathy** — confirm alignment before prescribing anything

## Question approach

Use **contextual** questions that speed up understanding without overwhelming the
buyer, rather than generic discovery lists.

**Impact and urgency are stronger when the buyer articulates them**, not the
seller.

## Earning the right to prescribe

Diagnosis strengthens qualification by validating understanding. The seller
earns the right to recommend through demonstrated empathy and relevant
third-party stories, before presenting a solution.

## Critical Events

Establishing a Critical Event clarifies priority and next steps. The urgency
should come from a milestone the buyer identifies, not a timeline the seller
imposes.

## Account-management relevance

The same sequence applies at a QBR or an expansion conversation: diagnose the
current situation and new pain before proposing an upsell.
