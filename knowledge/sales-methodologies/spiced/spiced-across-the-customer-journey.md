# SPICED Across the Customer Journey — Blueprint Summary

> **Partial source.** Stage-by-stage question sets are in the gated PDF.

## The problem it addresses

Poor handoffs erode trust and plant the seeds of churn long before the renewal
conversation. Customer context is lost each time an account changes hands, and
customers get frustrated repeating what they already told someone.

## SPICED as connective tissue

SPICED is used as a shared format for **capturing and transferring** customer
context across Sales, Customer Success and Account Management, so priorities and
expectations survive ownership changes.

## Critical insight

**Critical Events and impact goals must be revisited as customer priorities
change.** SPICED is not captured once at the sale; it is refined continuously
through the lifecycle.

## Ownership by role

| Role | SPICED responsibility |
|---|---|
| SDRs / AEs | Initial capture during awareness, education and selection |
| AEs | Complete handoff documentation before commit |
| CSMs / Account Managers | Maintain and update context through onboarding, adoption and expansion |
| RevOps / CS Operations | Standardize the documentation workflow |

## Outcomes

- Faster ramp-up for new account owners
- Smoother transitions
- Customers not asked to repeat information

## Account-management relevance (model note)

This is the source for treating SPICED as a **living account record** an AM
inherits and keeps current, rather than a pre-sale qualification artifact.
