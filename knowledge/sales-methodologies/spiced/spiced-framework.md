# SPICED — Framework Overview

## What it is

Winning by Design's framework for aligning revenue teams around how customers
actually make decisions. It gives Marketing, Sales and Customer Success a shared
language and structure for diagnosing what is working, what is broken, and where
to focus.

## The five elements

| Element | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **S — Situation** | The customer's current context: business environment, constraints, priorities, and the change triggers that explain why the problem exists now |
| **P — Pain** | The specific friction, inefficiency or risk the customer experiences as a result of that situation, described in concrete, observable terms |
| **I — Impact** | The meaningful consequences of solving or not solving the problem, in business terms — both **rational** outcomes and **emotional** stakes |
| **C — Critical Event** | The deadline, milestone or external force that creates urgency and determines when a decision must be made |
| **D — Decision** | How the customer chooses: the stakeholders, success criteria, trade-offs, and the process required to move forward |

## Why it exists

Traditional revenue processes lean on intuition and outdated stage definitions.
SPICED replaces that with a structure grounded in how buyers decide.

## What it gives an organization

- **A shared operating language** that reduces misalignment between teams
- **Clear customer context** — conversations anchored in situation and constraints
- **Opportunity discipline** — structured assessment of urgency, impact and the decision path
- **Consistent execution** across discovery, handoffs and deal strategy
- **Faster coaching** because managers coach against one diagnostic standard
- **More predictable outcomes** and forecast confidence

## Across the customer journey

SPICED is designed to persist as ownership of an account moves from Sales to
Customer Success to Account Management. It is the vehicle for capturing and
transferring customer context, so customers are not asked to repeat themselves.

## How Winning by Design positions it against other frameworks

- **MEDDIC/MEDDPICC** — strong on deal control, metrics, economic buyers and
  champions for enterprise complexity
- **BANT** — simpler and transactional, thin on impact and decision context
- **Command of the Message** — focused on differentiated narrative

Winning by Design positions SPICED as strengthening pipeline quality at entry
**and** post-close revenue health, and as framework-agnostic: it can layer into an
existing methodology and CRM.

## Operationalizing SPICED

From the SPICED Operating Model service page (largely promotional; limited
public detail):

- Map SPICED to CRM fields across stages
- Define **stage exit criteria** — what must be true, based on evidence, before an
  opportunity advances
- Configure conversation capture
- Equip managers to coach against one standard, with role-based training
- Apply the same five elements from awareness through expansion

Adoption works best when SPICED is treated as an operating model reinforced by
training, tooling and daily standards — not a one-off workshop.
