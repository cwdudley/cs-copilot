# The SPICED Framework — Blueprint Summary

> **Partial source.** The detailed question banks, examples, and documentation
> guidance are in a gated PDF that was not accessed.

## Core principle

Strong diagnosis requires more than finding pain. It requires understanding
**impact** and **urgency**.

## Elements as framed in this blueprint

- **Situation** — current-state context
- **Pain** — the specific problems
- **Impact** — business consequences of the pain
- **Critical Events** — the catalysts that set decision timing
- **Decision dynamics** — how, and by whom, the decision is made

## Key takeaways

1. **Impact and Critical Events are not discovered in sequence.** They surface
   throughout the conversation and require active listening rather than a
   scripted order.
2. **Decisions are made by groups.** The decision process has to be mapped
   explicitly, not assumed.
3. **Summarize and validate.** Playing SPICED back to the customer builds trust
   and momentum.
4. **Documented insight improves forecasting** and deal prioritization.
5. **It creates a shared language** across teams and lifecycle stages.

## Intended for

Sales leaders, AEs, SDRs, revenue leaders, Customer Success leaders, and
enablement teams — especially organizations with weak qualification or
late-stage surprises.
